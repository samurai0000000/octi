Chapter 1 — Board Geometry, Pods, Prongs & Starting Setup

OCTI is a deterministic, perfect-information abstract strategy game of vector construction. Every pod begins blank (with zero movement directions) and earns mobility as players insert directional prongs one turn at a time.

  • 9×9 Standard Grid: Columns x = 1..9 run West-to-East (left to right); rows y = 1..9 run South-to-North (bottom to top). Every square is written as two digits xy (for example, 53 is column 5, row 3).
  • 2-Player Starting Setup (2P_3B & 2P_1B): Player A starts with 3 pods on home OCTI bases 33, 53, and 73; Player B starts with 3 pods on home OCTI bases 37, 57, and 77. Each player also holds 4 reserve pods (7 pods total per side) and 25 prongs (or 24 normal prongs + 1 gold SuperProng in _SP variants).
  • 8 Compass Prong Sockets (a–h): Every octagonal pod has 8 perimeter sockets mapped clockwise from North: a=N (0,+1), b=NE (+1,+1), c=E (+1,0), d=SE (+1,-1), e=S (0,-1), f=SW (-1,-1), g=W (-1,0), and h=NW (-1,+1).

Chapter 2 — Complete Turn Actions

Player A moves first, and turns alternate strictly (in 4P, turn order rotates A → B → C → D, skipping eliminated seats). On your turn, you must execute exactly one of the five regular turn actions below (no passing is permitted):

  1. Insert a Prong (xy<cfg>+<dir>): Take 1 prong from your supply and insert it into an empty socket on one of your pods on the board (e.g., 55acg+b; or 33+b! for a gold SuperProng).
  2. Reposition a Normal Prong (xy<cfg>+<add>-<rm>): Move an existing normal prong on one of your pods to another empty socket on that same pod (e.g., 33b+a-b). Costs 0 supply prongs. Forbidden on SuperProngs (!) and forbidden in OCTI for Kids (OFK).
  3. 1-Square Step (xy<cfg>-x'y'): Move a pod 1 square along one of its installed prong directions onto an empty square or a friendly/allied stack (e.g., 44ab-45). A 1-square step never captures and cannot enter a square occupied by an enemy pod.
  4. 2-Square Jump & Multi-Hop Chain (xy<cfg>-x1y1[x]-x2y2[x]...): Jump 2 squares along an installed prong direction over an adjacent occupied square (friendly or enemy) onto an empty landing square beyond it. You may chain additional jumps with that same pod in any of its installed prong directions (pivoting mid-chain), and you may stop early after any legal hop.
  5. Deploy Reserve Pod (xy) or Liberate Captured Pod (xy*): Place 1 pod from reserve onto a non-enemy-occupied own home base (or an enemy base you already occupy), or liberate 1 of your captured pods (xy*) onto an OCTI square you already occupy when you hold Liberation Clearance.
Optional Capture & Prong Plunder: Jumping over an enemy square makes capture optional (append x to capture, e.g., 44ab-66x, or omit x to leave the jumped enemy stack intact, e.g., 44ab-66). Every pod on a captured square is removed at the end of the turn: its pod body goes to its owner's capturedPods jail, while all prongs attached to it transfer immediately into the capturing player's free prong supply! Friendly/allied pods can be jumped over freely and are never captured.

Chapter 3 — Stacking, Composite Moves & Appendix A Edge Cases

  • Friendly Stacking & Composite Multi-Pod Moves (,): Multiple friendly pods (and diagonal-partner pods in 4P) may stack on the same square (forbidden in OFK). Any subset of your own pods that start the turn on the same square may move together or split along separate step/jump paths in a single turn, joined by commas (e.g., 44a-45,44beg-34). Pod placement and prong edits can never be combined with movement.
  • End-of-Turn Capture Removal: Jumped enemy stacks marked with x remain physically on the board as jumpable fulcrums for remaining hops in the same turn and are removed together when the turn finishes. However, a single pod may never jump over the exact same square twice during one hop chain.
  • Appendix A.1 — Starting-Square Vacancy & Loop-Back Jumps: During a single or composite move from square S, all pods initially on S (including stationary stack-mates!) are treated as vacated for jump geometry: a jumping pod from S cannot jump over S, yet can legally land back onto S at the end of a multi-hop loop (e.g., 44beg-66x-64x-44 rejoining stationary A44a on 44!).
  • Liberation Clearance Exact Conditions: Liberating a captured pod (xy*) always requires the target OCTI square to be already occupied by at least one of your own pods (never an empty base!). In 2P, liberating onto your own home base additionally requires occupying at least one enemy base (canLiberate); liberating directly onto an enemy base you already occupy is always legal whenever capturedPods > 0. In 4P, liberating your own captured pod onto your own or active partner's base requires personally occupying a neutral (53/57) or enemy OCTI square.
  • Edgeless Torus Wrapping (_ED): Column 1 wraps to column 9 and row 1 wraps to row 9 (including diagonals) for both 1-square steps (15g-95) and 2-square jumps (97b-29x jumping B18e).
  • SuperProngs & Bonus Preludes (_SP, ;): A gold SuperProng (!) is permanent once inserted (cannot be repositioned). Before your regular turn action, you may execute 1 or 2 bonus prelude moves delimited by semicolons (e.g., 44ab!-66x;66ab!-67). Each prelude must move along a single SuperProng socket (1-square step or single-direction jump chain), and its captures resolve immediately before the next prelude or regular action! The exact same SuperProng socket on the exact same pod cannot be fired twice in two preludes on the same turn.

Chapter 4 — Victory, Elimination & Stalemate Conditions

  • 2P_3B Family (3-Base Standard): Win immediately at the end of your turn if your pods simultaneously occupy all 3 enemy home bases (37, 57, 77 for A; 33, 53, 73 for B).
  • 2P_1B Family (1-Base Blitz): Win immediately when your pod occupies any 1 of the 3 enemy home bases.
  • OFK (OCTI for Kids, 6×7): Win immediately when your pod occupies any 1 of the 4 enemy home bases (26, 36, 46, 56 for A; 22, 32, 42, 52 for B).
  • 4P Partnership Family (Team A&C vs. Team B&D): Diagonal partners win together when allied pods simultaneously occupy both enemy corner bases (37 and 73 for Team A&C; 33 and 77 for Team B&D).
  • 4-Player Base-Capture Elimination & Teammate Revival: In 4P, if an enemy pod ends its turn on your corner home base (or you have zero pods left on the board and zero legal placements), you are eliminated: all of your pods on the board are swept into your capturedPods pool and their prongs are forfeited to the capturing side. Your surviving partner plays on and can revive you by moving one of their own pods onto your home base (once clear of enemies) and playing xy* (e.g., 77*), restoring 1 of your captured pods to the board!
  • Total Wipeout / Stalemate: If the next player (or both enemy seats in 4P) has zero legal moves (for example, all pods captured and no legal pod placement available), the player/team who just moved wins immediately. Timed games also end on clock flag forfeit or resignation.

OCTI-FEN Position String Format

Board states serialize into colon-separated OCTI-FEN strings (11 tokens for 2 / OFK; 13 tokens for 4):

mode:bases:super:edgeless:status:turn:winner:elimMask:resA:resB[:resC:resD]:squares

  • mode: 2, 4, or OFK • bases: 3 or 1 • super / edgeless: 0 or 1.
  • status: N (not started), P (in progress), O (game over), R (resigned).
  • turn / winner: A, B, C, D, or - • elimMask: 4P bitmask (A=1, B=2, C=4, D=8).
  • resP: freePods,capturedPods,freeProngs,freeSuperProngs (e.g., 4,0,25,0).
  • squares: comma-separated pods in row-major order <Owner><x><y><prongs> (e.g., A44ab!,B55e!), or - if empty.

Chapter 7 — Dual-Pool Elo & Competitive Arena Rules

Official ratings start at 1200 Elo on three independent 1v1 variant ladders: 2P_3B, 2P_1B, and OFK. All 4-Player (4P*), SuperProng (*_SP), Edgeless (*_ED), combined (*_SPED), and offline matches are strictly casual (unrated).

Pool 1 — Human-Only Ladders (Leaderboard & Players Roster):
Tracks exclusively online rated Human-vs-Human matches using standard Elo factor K = 32. Human-Only ratings are 100% insulated from bot matches.
Pool 2 — Open Bot & Human Arena (Robots Directory):
Tracks rated online matches involving at least one registered AI robot (Human-vs-Robot and Robot-vs-Robot) on a unified competitive scale with four anti-farming safeguards:
  • Cross-Owner Matches: Standard K = 32.
  • Same-Owner Bot-vs-Bot Self-Testing: Allowed at reduced K = 16.
  • Human-vs-Own-Robot Exclusion: Always forced unrated to prevent manual point transfers.
  • Pairwise Frequency & Early-Resign Guards: At most 4 rated games per rolling 24-hour window between any unordered participant pair (5th+ auto-downgrades to casual), and resignations before 8 plies record 0 Elo delta.
