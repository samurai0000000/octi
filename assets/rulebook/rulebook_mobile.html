Chapter 1 — Board Geometry, Pods, Prongs & Starting Setup

OCTI is a deterministic, perfect-information abstract strategy game of vector construction. Every pod enters the board blank (with zero movement directions) and earns directional mobility as players insert prongs one turn at a time.

  • 9×9 Standard Grid: Columns x = 1..9 run West-to-East (left to right); rows y = 1..9 run South-to-North (bottom to top). Every square is written as two digits xy (for example, 53 is column 5, row 3).
  • 2-Player Starting Setup (2P_3B & 2P_1B): Player A starts with 3 pods on home OCTI bases 33, 53, and 73; Player B starts with 3 pods on home OCTI bases 37, 57, and 77. Each player also holds 4 reserve pods (7 pods total per side) and 25 prongs (or 24 normal prongs + 1 gold SuperProng in _SP variants).
  • 8 Compass Prong Sockets (a–h): Every octagonal pod has 8 perimeter sockets mapped clockwise from North: a=N (0,+1), b=NE (+1,+1), c=E (+1,0), d=SE (+1,-1), e=S (0,-1), f=SW (-1,-1), g=W (-1,0), and h=NW (-1,+1).

Chapter 2 — Complete Turn Actions

Player A moves first, and turns alternate strictly (in 4P, turn order rotates A → B → C → D, skipping eliminated seats). Passing is forbidden; on your turn, you must execute exactly one regular turn action:

  1. Insert a Prong (xy<cfg>+<dir>): Take 1 prong from your free supply and insert it into an empty socket on one of your pods on the board (e.g., 55acg+b; or 33+b! for a gold SuperProng). Once inserted, prongs are permanent on that pod—repositioning an installed prong to another socket is never allowed in any variant.
  2. 1-Square Step (xy<cfg>-x'y'): Move a pod 1 square along one of its installed prong directions onto an empty square or a friendly/allied stack (e.g., 44ab-45). A 1-square step never captures and cannot enter a square occupied by an enemy pod.
  3. 2-Square Jump & Multi-Hop Chain (xy<cfg>-x1y1[x]-x2y2[x]...): Jump 2 squares along an installed prong direction over an adjacent occupied square (friendly or enemy) onto an empty landing square beyond it. You may chain additional jumps with that same pod in any of its installed prong directions (pivoting mid-chain), and you may stop early after any legal hop.
  4. Deploy Reserve Pod (xy) or Liberate Captured Pod (xy*): Place 1 reserve pod onto a non-enemy-occupied own home base (or an enemy base you already occupy), or—in 3-Base (2P_3B*) and 4-Player (4P*) games—liberate 1 of your own captured pods (xy*) onto an OCTI square you already occupy when eligible (liberation does not apply to 1-Base sudden-death variants or OFK).
Optional Whole-Stack Capture & Prong Plunder: Jumping over an occupied square makes capture optional: append x (e.g., 44ab-66x) to capture all pods in the jumped stack (whole-stack capture—selective or partial stack capture is never allowed), or omit x (e.g., 44ab-66) to leave the jumped stack intact. During regular single or composite moves, captured stacks are removed at the end of the turn into each pod owner's capturedPods pool while all prongs attached to them transfer into the capturing player's free prong supply! Friendly/allied pods may be vaulted freely without capture.

Chapter 3 — Stacking, Composite Moves & Appendix A Edge Cases

  • Friendly Stacking & Composite Multi-Pod Moves (,): Multiple friendly pods (and diagonal-partner pods in 4P) may stack on the same square (forbidden in OFK). Any subset of your own pods that start the turn on the same square may move together or split along separate step/jump paths in a single turn, joined by commas (e.g., 44a-45,44beg-34). Pod placement and prong insertion never combine with movement.
  • Whole-Stack Capture & End-of-Turn Removal: Jumping over an occupied square with x captures every pod in that square's stack. In regular single and composite moves, stacks jumped with x remain physically on the board as jumpable fulcrums for remaining composite stack-mates during the same turn and are removed together at turn end. A single pod may never jump over the exact same square twice during one hop chain.
  • Appendix A.1 — Starting-Square Vacancy, Loop-Back Jumps & Stacked-Pod Non-Jumping: For the duration of its jump chain, a jumping pod cannot jump over its starting square S or any pod with which it started stacked (so an Edgeless _ED wraparound jump such as 44bcd-66-86-16-34-54 over stationary A44a on 44 is illegal). Furthermore, a jumping pod may land back onto S at the end of a multi-hop loop only when all pods that started the turn on S vacate S during the move (for example, composite clear-out 44a-45,44beg-66x-64x-44 is legal because 44 is completely vacated, whereas single 44beg-66x-64x-44 leaving stationary A44a on 44 is illegal).
  • Pod Liberation Scope & Clearance (2P_3B* & 4P*): Liberating a captured pod (xy*) applies exclusively to 3-Base (2P_3B*) and 4-Player (4P*) games (never 1-Base sudden death or OFK), always liberates 1 of your own captured pods (capturedPods > 0), and always requires the target OCTI square to be already occupied by at least one of your own pods (never an empty or partner-only square). In 2P_3B*, liberating onto your own home base additionally requires occupying at least one enemy base; liberating directly onto an enemy base you already occupy requires only capturedPods > 0. In 4P*, liberating onto your own or partner's corner base requires personally occupying a neutral (53/57) or enemy OCTI square (even if your partner is eliminated, xy* on their corner base liberates your own pod and never restores an eliminated partner).
  • Edgeless Torus Wrapping (_ED): Column 1 wraps to column 9 and row 1 wraps to row 9 (including diagonals) for both 1-square steps (15g-95) and 2-square jumps (97b-29x jumping B18e).
  • SuperProngs & Bonus Preludes (_SP, ;): A gold SuperProng (!) is permanent once inserted. Before your regular turn action, you may execute 1 bonus prelude move per SuperProng you control on the board (up to 2 in 2P_SP and up to 4 in 4P_SP when enemy SuperProngs are captured), delimited by semicolons (e.g., 44ab!-66x;66ab!-67). Each prelude moves 1 pod strictly in a straight line (1-square step or collinear jump chain, no pivoting) along a distinct SuperProng socket that has not yet fired a bonus prelude this turn. Stacks jumped with x during a SuperProng bonus prelude are removed immediately after that prelude before the next SuperProng prelude or regular action begins! A pod that moved in a bonus prelude may still use any of its prongs in the regular turn phase, and victory is checked only at completion of the full turn.

Chapter 4 — Victory, Elimination & Stalemate Conditions

  • 2P_3B Family (3-Base Standard): Win at the completion of your full turn if your pods simultaneously occupy all 3 enemy home bases (37, 57, 77 for A; 33, 53, 73 for B).
  • 2P_1B Family (1-Base Blitz): Win at the completion of your full turn when your pod occupies any 1 of the 3 enemy home bases.
  • OFK (OCTI for Kids, 6×7): Win immediately when your pod occupies any 1 of the 4 enemy home bases (26, 36, 46, 56 for A; 22, 32, 42, 52 for B).
  • 4P Partnership Family (Team A&C vs. Team B&D): Diagonal partners win together at the completion of a full turn when allied pods simultaneously occupy both enemy corner bases (37 and 73 for Team A&C; 33 and 77 for Team B&D).
  • 4-Player Permanent Base-Capture Elimination: In 4P, if an enemy pod ends its full turn on your corner home base (or you have zero pods left on the board and zero legal placements), you are permanently eliminated for the remainder of the game: all of your remaining board pods are swept into your capturedPods pool, and all prongs (normal and SuperProngs) from your swept board pods transfer to the capturing enemy player. Eliminated seats are skipped in turn rotation while a surviving partner plays on.
  • Total Wipeout / Stalemate: Because prong repositioning is forbidden, a player with zero reserve pods and zero reserve prongs whose board pods are completely boxed in has zero legal moves. If the next player in 2P / OFK (or both enemy seats in 4P) has zero legal moves, the player/team who just moved wins immediately (in 4P, a stalemated player whose partner still has a legal move has their turn skipped). Timed games also end on clock flag forfeit or resignation.

OCTI-FEN Position String Format

Board states serialize into colon-separated OCTI-FEN strings (11 tokens for 2 / OFK; 13 tokens for 4):

mode:bases:super:edgeless:status:turn:winner:elimMask:resA:resB[:resC:resD]:squares

  • mode: 2, 4, or OFK • bases: 3 or 1 • super / edgeless: 0 or 1.
  • status: N (not started), P (in progress), O (game over), R (resigned).
  • turn / winner: A, B, C, D, or - • elimMask: 4P bitmask (A=1, B=2, C=4, D=8).
  • resP: freePods,capturedPods,freeProngs,freeSuperProngs (e.g., 4,0,25,0).
  • squares: comma-separated pods in row-major order <Owner><x><y><prongs> (e.g., A44ab!,B55e!), or - if empty.

Chapter 7 — Dual-Pool Elo & Competitive Arena Rules

Official ratings start at 1200 Elo on three independent 1v1 variant ladders: 2P_3B, 2P_1B, and OFK. All 4-Player (4P*), SuperProng (*_SP), Edgeless (*_ED), combined (*_SPED), and offline matches are strictly casual (unrated).

Pool 1 — Human-Only Ladders (Leaderboard & Players Roster):
Tracks exclusively online rated Human-vs-Human matches using standard Elo factor K = 32. Human-Only ratings are 100% insulated from bot matches.
Pool 2 — Open Bot & Human Arena (Leaderboard & Robots Directory):
Tracks rated online matches involving at least one registered AI robot (Human-vs-Robot and Robot-vs-Robot) on a unified competitive scale with three anti-farming safeguards:
  • Human-vs-Robot Matches: Unlimited rated play at standard K = 32 (including against your own robots).
  • Bot-vs-Bot K-Factors: Cross-owner matches use standard K = 32; same-owner self-testing uses reduced K = 16.
  • Bot-vs-Bot Pair Cap & Early-Resign Guards: Bot-vs-Bot matches are capped at 4 rated games per rolling 24-hour window between any unordered bot pair (5th+ auto-downgrades to casual; no daily cap across distinct opponent bots), and resignations before 8 plies record 0 Elo delta.
