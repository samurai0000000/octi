## Privacy and Consent

Game records submitted to or archived by the OCTI server:
1. Contain no personal identifiable information (PII).
2. Omit any persistent device identifiers (`installId`), exact client IP addresses, or location data from public APIs and exports.
3. Include game metadata (mode, ruleset, ply moves, elapsed move times, AI search statistics, and anonymous participant handles).
